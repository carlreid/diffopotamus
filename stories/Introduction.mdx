**Powerful image comparison with the precision of a pixel-peeping hippo!**

<Canvas of={AllPluginsStories.AllPlugins} />

The best way to understand Diffopotamus is to see it working. Choose a comparison method or inspect an individual image:

- **[All Plugins](?path=/story/diffopotamus-all-plugins--all-plugins)** - Explore comparison and inspection side by side
- **[Slider](?path=/story/diffopotamus-plugins-slider-plugin--default)** - Interactive reveal with drag controls  
- **[Side by Side](?path=/story/diffopotamus-plugins-side-by-side-plugin--default)** - Traditional split view
- **[Overlay](?path=/story/diffopotamus-plugins-overlay-plugin--default)** - Smooth blending transitions
- **[Lightbox](?path=/story/diffopotamus-plugins-lightbox-plugin--default)** - Inspect one image with zoom, pan, and temporary pen/rectangle marks
- **[Single Image](?path=/story/diffopotamus-plugins-lightbox-plugin--single-image)** - Lightbox with one image input and no Before/After selector
